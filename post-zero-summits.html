Zero summits (so far)

This is a website about climbing mountains, made by someone who hasn't climbed one.

Most mountain sites show the finished thing: the summit photo, the gear list, the numbers. I'd rather keep the whole trail visible, including the part where nothing has happened yet. That part is where most people are, and it's where I am.

The plan is small. Pick a real hike. Go. Write down what went wrong. Pick a harder one. The peaks page has the big mountains, but the first checkmark will probably be something with a parking lot.

If you've climbed things, I'd like to hear what you wish you knew at the start. Sign the guestbook.
